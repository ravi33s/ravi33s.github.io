Dr. Ravi Sharma is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Built Environment at Eindhoven University of Technology (TU/e), Netherlands, where he is an active member of the WILSON Horizon Europe Project. His research focuses on Industry 4.0, the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT), Industrial Cyber-Physical Systems (ICPS), wireless sensor networks (WSNs), and computer networking.

He earned his PhD (4.96/5 CGPA) from Budapest University of Technology and Economics (BME), Hungary, where his doctoral research focused on the Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) and Industry 4.0 in Enterprise Resource Planning. He received his Master's degree in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT), Patna, India. His work aims to reduce human intervention in data flow from industrial plants to ERP and cloud infrastructure by implementing cutting-edge techniques including secure query processing, sensor device integration, and trustworthy code dissemination.

"We are currently in the midst of a technological and computing revolution that will drastically change our lives and potentially redefine what it means to be human. I am very excited at the prospect of devoting my long-term career to such a dynamic, fast advancing field."
