Ravi Sharma is a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Built Environment at Eindhoven University of Technology, The Netherlands. Driven by the rapid evolution of digital infrastructure, his work focuses on transforming industrial and built environment data into secure, trustworthy, and sustainable systems. Currently, he is working on the WILSON Horizon Europe Project, where he develops distributed data models, semantic ontologies, and secure data-exchange architectures designed to optimize the lifecycles and environmental footprints of modern buildings and districts.

His academic journey is anchored in bridging the gap between hardware sensors and cloud enterprise architectures. He earned his doctorate from the Budapest University of Technology and Economics, Hungary, where his research advanced privacy, security, and automated data paths for Industrial IoT and Industry 4.0 applications. His current research applies this expertise to the AECO (Architecture, Engineering, Construction, and Operations) domain, exploring the intersections of federated digital twins, cyber-physical systems, and blockchain-enabled infrastructure to build the interconnected data spaces of tomorrow.
