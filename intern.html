We are always looking for self-motivated, passionate individuals who can think independently, come up with their own ideas, step out of their comfort zone and plunge into the exciting field of Privacy and Security in Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT). However, currently there are no open positions!

Our research lab is looking for interns with a solid foundation in programming, deep learning, and natural language processing/computational linguistics. Candidates who can showcase their coding skills through a GitHub portfolio will be given preference. Applicants interested in pursuing research should submit a cover letter detailing their motivation and interest in contributing to our ongoing projects (see the Project page for more information). A commitment of at least 4 months is required, with preference given to onsite candidates.
