Horizon Europe May 2024 – Apr 2028

WILSON: Distributed Data Modelling and Federated Digital Twinning for Lifecycle Data-driven Sustainable Operation and Management of Buildings and Districts

WILSON focuses on distributed data modelling and federated digital twinning for lifecycle data-driven sustainable operation and management of buildings and districts. The project develops cutting-edge approaches to information systems in the built environment.

PhD Thesis Sep 2019 – Feb 2024

Research of Industrial Internet of Things and Industry 4.0 in Enterprise Resource Planning

PhD thesis at Budapest University of Technology and Economics. Research accomplishments have significant implications for the advancement of Industrial IoT, Industry 4.0, and related domains, paving the way for future research and development. Topics include spatiotemporal query processing, secure code dissemination, predictive analytics for smart manufacturing, and data management architectures.

DAAD-DST Feb 2015 – Feb 2017

Time Synchronization in Duty Cycled Wireless Sensor Networks

DST-DAAD Indo-German Project Based Personnel Exchange Programme. The goal was to model broadcast duration to ensure that a certain percentage of available nodes are synchronized. The problem of estimating broadcast duration is formulated to capture the probability threshold of nodes being synchronized.
Grant: #INT/FRG/DAAD/P-250/2015

Team: Prof. Dr. Ashok Singh Sairam (PI), Prof. Dr.-Ing. Axel Sikora (PI), Akash Yadav, Ravi Sharma
